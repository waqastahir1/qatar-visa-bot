============================================================
  QATAR VISA BOT — HOW TO USE
============================================================

WHAT THIS APP DOES
------------------
This app monitors the Qatar Visa Center website (qatarvisacenter.com)
and automatically books an appointment slot the moment one becomes
available. You control everything from a dashboard in your browser.


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — START THE APP
------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click:  start.bat

This opens two terminal windows (backend + frontend) and automatically
launches the dashboard in your browser at:

  http://localhost:3000

Keep both terminal windows open while using the app.
Closing them will shut down the app.


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — OPEN SETTINGS
------------------------------------------------------------
Click the Settings icon (top-right of the dashboard).

Fill in the following:

  Qatar Visa Center Login
  -----------------------
  - Email Address   : The email you use to log in to qatarvisacenter.com
  - Password        : Your QVC account password

  Monitoring Date Range
  ---------------------
  - Start Date : The earliest date you want to book (e.g. 2025-11-01)
  - End Date   : The latest date you want to book   (e.g. 2025-12-31)

  Bot Behavior
  ------------
  - Check Interval (seconds)  : How often the bot checks for slots (default: 60)
  - Months to Monitor         : How many months ahead to scan (default: 2)
  - CAPTCHA Wait Time (sec)   : How long the bot pauses for you to solve
                                a CAPTCHA manually (default: 60)
  - Stealth Mode              : ON = human-like random delays (recommended)
  - Auto Book                 : ON = bot books the slot automatically when found
  - Auto Click Next           : ON = bot clicks the Next button automatically
  - Auto Click Confirm        : ON = bot clicks the Confirm button automatically

  Gmail Email Notifications (Optional)
  -------------------------------------
  If you want to receive email alerts when a slot is found, booked,
  or when a CAPTCHA appears:

  1. Enable Email Alerts       : Turn this ON
  2. Your Gmail Address        : The Gmail account that will SEND the alerts
                                 (e.g. yourname@gmail.com)
  3. Gmail App Password        : A special password for this app (NOT your
                                 normal Gmail password). To get one:
                                   a. Go to myaccount.google.com
                                   b. Security → Enable 2-Step Verification
                                   c. Security → App Passwords
                                   d. Create one for "Mail"
                                   e. Copy the 16-character code here
  4. Recipient Email           : The email address that will RECEIVE the alerts
                                 (can be the same as your Gmail address)

Click SAVE SETTINGS when done.


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — ADD YOUR CLIENTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Clients are the people whose appointments you want to book.
If you are booking only for yourself, add yourself as one client.

Click "Add Client" and fill in:
  - Full Name      : Applicant's full name
  - Passport No.   : Passport number (used to fill the booking form)
  - Visa Type      : Type of visa being applied for
  - Mobile         : Contact phone number
  - Email          : Contact email
  - Priority       : Urgent = booked first, Normal = booked after urgent ones

Click Save. You can add multiple clients.
The bot will book them in order of priority (Urgent first).


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — START THE BOT
------------------------------------------------------------
Click the START BOT button on the dashboard.

A Chromium browser window will open — this is normal.
The bot uses this visible browser to navigate the QVC website.
Do NOT close this browser window while the bot is running.

The bot will:
  1. Log in to your QVC account
  2. Navigate to the appointment booking page
  3. Check available slots within your date range
  4. If a slot is found → attempt to book it for your top-priority client
  5. Repeat every X seconds (based on your Check Interval setting)

You can watch live logs at the bottom of the dashboard.


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — HANDLING CAPTCHA
------------------------------------------------------------
Sometimes the QVC website shows a CAPTCHA (image puzzle).
The bot cannot solve this automatically — you must do it manually.

When a CAPTCHA appears:
  1. You will receive an email alert (if Gmail is configured)
  2. The log will show "CAPTCHA detected — please solve manually"
  3. Switch to the Chromium browser window
  4. Solve the CAPTCHA yourself
  5. The bot will wait (based on your CAPTCHA Wait Time setting)
     and then continue automatically


------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — STOP THE BOT
------------------------------------------------------------
Click the STOP BOT button on the dashboard at any time.
The Chromium browser will close and monitoring will stop.


------------------------------------------------------------
EMAIL ALERTS YOU WILL RECEIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
If Gmail notifications are enabled, you will get emails for:

  - Bot Started         : Bot has begun monitoring
  - Bot Stopped         : Bot has been stopped
  - Slot Found          : An available slot was detected
  - Appointment Booked  : A slot was successfully booked (includes details)
  - Booking Failed      : A booking attempt failed (bot will keep trying)
  - CAPTCHA Detected    : Manual action required from you


------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Set a shorter Check Interval (e.g. 30 seconds) if slots fill up fast
  - Keep the app running overnight — slots often appear at odd hours
  - Make sure your QVC account is valid and not locked before starting
  - The bot opens a real browser — your screen may show it in the taskbar
  - If the bot crashes, check the terminal window for error messages
    and report them for support


------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------
  App does not open at http://localhost:3000
    → Make sure both terminal windows from start.bat are still open
    → Wait 10 seconds after running start.bat and try again

  Bot fails to log in
    → Double-check your QVC email and password in Settings
    → Try logging in manually at qatarvisacenter.com to confirm they work

  No slots found
    → The website may genuinely have no slots in your date range
    → Try widening your date range or increasing Months to Monitor

  CAPTCHA keeps appearing
    → Enable Stealth Mode in Settings to reduce CAPTCHA frequency
    → Increase the Check Interval so the bot checks less often

  Email alerts not arriving
    → Confirm your Gmail App Password is correct (not your regular password)
    → Check your spam/junk folder
    → Make sure 2-Step Verification is enabled on your Google account
